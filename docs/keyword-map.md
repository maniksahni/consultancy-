## What moves rankings from here (cannot be done in code)

1. **Google Business Profile** for Althéa Consulting (Paris) — biggest lever for branded and local results.
2. **Real backlinks / mentions**: Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube profiles linking to the site (then add them to `brand.socialLinks` → `sameAs`), Indian student forums, guest posts, alumni groups.
3. **Reviews** on Google Business Profile from real students.
4. **Fresh first-hand content** every 2–4 weeks: e.g. Campus France interview questions, real budget breakdown for Paris, alternance search walkthrough. AI engines cite specific, first-hand, dated answers.
5. Track in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools (AI Performance) monthly.

No one can guarantee a #1 ranking; these steps maximise the chance.
