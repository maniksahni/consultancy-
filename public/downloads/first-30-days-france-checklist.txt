FIRST 30 DAYS IN FRANCE — STUDENT ARRIVAL CHECKLIST

Use this as a planning aid. Requirements vary by city, institution, nationality and personal circumstances. Confirm current instructions with the relevant official organisation. This checklist is general guidance, not legal, immigration, banking or housing advice.

BEFORE AND JUST AFTER ARRIVAL
[ ] Keep passport, admission and accommodation records together
[ ] Save emergency, university and accommodation contact details
[ ] Arrange a French SIM or another reliable way to stay connected
[ ] Check local transport options, passes and routes

FIRST TWO WEEKS
[ ] Ask your bank which documents it needs; requirements vary
[ ] Check your institution's arrival, enrolment and attendance instructions
[ ] Review health insurance and student administration steps
[ ] Keep copies and dates for official paperwork and appointments

FIRST MONTH
[ ] If eligible, read current CAF housing-aid information and gather questions
[ ] Learn local routines: shops, healthcare access and transport
[ ] Update your CV and LinkedIn with a France-ready direction
[ ] Choose one realistic study, language or career goal for the next month
