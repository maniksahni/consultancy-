## FAQ

Headline: **Good questions. Clear answers.**
Intro: Understand the approach before you decide whether it's right for you.

**What does one-to-one mentorship include?**
Direct guidance through profile review, shortlisting, application preparation, and visa preparation. Your senior mentor helps you understand what to prepare and how each stage connects.

**How do you keep shortlisting independent?**
We accept no university recruitment commissions. We discuss options in relation to your academic profile, budget, interests, and plans.

**Do you guarantee admission or visa approval?**
No. Universities and the relevant authorities make those decisions. Mentorship helps you prepare and understand the process; it cannot guarantee the result.

**Can you help if I have an education gap or a previous refusal?**
We can review your circumstances and the available supporting documents with you. What happens next depends on your individual case and the requirements that apply. A previous decision cannot be promised to change.
