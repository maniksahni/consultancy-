# Student information review — 14 October 2026

## Published scope

The knowledge hub contains 59 source-linked summaries and planning entries across seven countries. Country pages include admissions, programme research, costs, scholarships, visa preparation, work, graduation and arrival resources. India-specific France funding and German APS/dMAT updates are included.

This is a manual editorial snapshot. It does not certify every programme, deadline, fee, scholarship opportunity or applicant’s immigration eligibility. Programme catalogues are linked to publishers rather than represented as a complete mirrored database. No recurring automatic policy monitor was created.

## Evidence labels

- Official page reviewed: the relevant official page was retrieved during the review.
- Official search listing: the official publisher’s indexed search result supported the concise summary; the full page was not always retrievable. These entries do not claim a complete page review.
- Planning checklist: editorial preparation steps rather than verified personalised eligibility or guaranteed requirements.

Review dates are separate from rule effective dates. Future changes such as the UK Graduate route duration from January 2027 are labelled with their applicable date.

## Important reviewed changes and conditions

- UK maintenance requirements and Graduate route application-date rules: GOV.UK.
- Canada’s September 2026 living-funds threshold, public graduate PAL/TAL exemption, PGWP language/programme conditions: IRCC.
- Australia’s July 2026 Student visa fee, financial capacity, insurance, work and graduate visa conditions: Study Australia.
- Germany’s 2026 financial requirement, employment and graduate search route: the Federal Government’s Make it in Germany portal.
- APS India’s qualification and upcoming dMAT information: APS India.
- Ireland’s financial evidence, work conditions, provider-list transition and graduate permission resources: Immigration Service Delivery.
- France application guidance and India funding: Campus France India. Several central Campus France tuition, Eiffel and housing pages could only be reviewed through official indexed listings and are labelled accordingly.
- US application, funding and visa resources: EducationUSA and US State Department official listings. Employment training guidance is linked to DHS; no blanket employment entitlement is asserted.

## Product checks

Filters expose country and topic summaries. The downloadable text guide includes the selected results, review date, source URLs, evidence labels and preparation checklist. The budget calculator uses user-entered values only and explicitly does not perform currency conversion or determine visa eligibility.
