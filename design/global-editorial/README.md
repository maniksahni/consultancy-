# Althéa — Parisian at heart, global in possibility

The site uses ivory, deep French navy, champagne accents, Cormorant Garamond and Plus Jakarta Sans. A full-width Paris photograph introduces a global study journey. Editorial sections balance imagery, typography and practical information.

## Student experience

- Seven destinations: France, UK, USA, Canada, Germany, Australia and Ireland.
- Interactive destination atlas with region filters, a three-destination browser-local shortlist and side-by-side planning comparison.
- Individual country pages with imagery, study planning, application context, official research resources and preparation checklists.
- Country-to-consultation links preselect the destination. A WhatsApp link provides a direct contact alternative while secure form delivery remains unconfigured.
- Existing France programme examples, university discovery, government institution registry and Campus France guides remain accessible at their published routes.

## Content constraints

France is the specialist advisory focus. Other destinations are research and enquiry experiences; adviser availability and scope must be confirmed. Country cards do not invent success rates, partnerships, tuition figures, scholarships or visa outcomes. Official resources control current requirements.

## Validation

The 9 Playwright test groups passed, covering exported routes at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels; additional homepage sizes; image loading; navigation, course filters, FAQ and consultation states; shortlist persistence; destination comparison; country preselection; internal link resolution; and WCAG A/AA checks on mobile and desktop.

Generated font class names were initially inconsistent with cached CSS. A clean production build fixed the mismatch, and the browser verified that the intended Jakarta font loaded. Text contrast issues identified by axe were corrected.
